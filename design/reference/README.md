**Style only.** Take the structure, the print techniques and the rhythm. Never their copy, logos, photos, product or colours. CLAUDE.md §11.1 says exactly what comes from each.

The four files are other people's work, so they are not published with this repository (owner, Nov 14). The owner keeps them outside git, in `~/samite-design-reference/`.

| File | Source | What it gave us |

| `ref-2-park.png` | Park | The square-dot print of a figure beside a headline (the "Built for machines first" section) |
